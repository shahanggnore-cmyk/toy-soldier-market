Customer Model

The sample simulation uses four customer types. Exact budgets and product values are configured privately pending instructor approval.

  • Bargain hunters: lower willingness to pay and a strict budget; may choose to buy nothing.
  • Collectors: higher values for desired products and a possible benefit from prior seller relationships.
  • Convenience shoppers: inspect a limited, randomized subset of sellers and account for inconvenience.
  • Returning customers: retain a stable identity and seller purchase history across simulated days.

Choice rule: utility = product value − total price + loyalty benefit − inconvenience cost. Customers choose the highest positive-utility eligible offer they can afford and that has virtual stock, or buy nothing. Exact ties are resolved using the saved random seed.
